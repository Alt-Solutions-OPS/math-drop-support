How to play

Math problems fall inside glass drops. Type the answer of any drop on the keypad and press ENTER before it reaches the water. Three misses end the run. Faster answers earn a bigger speed bonus, and correct answers in a row build a combo multiplier (up to ×3); a wrong answer or a miss resets it.

Pick a starting difficulty on the main menu: Easy (addition and subtraction), Medium (adds multiplication), Hard or Expert (all four operations with bigger numbers). Drops speed up as you level up, and strong runs move up to the next difficulty.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts.

Where are my scores saved? Only on your device, with one best score per starting difficulty. Deleting the app deletes your best scores and settings.

How do I turn off music, sound effects or vibration? Use the switches on the main menu or in the pause menu. Reduce motion is there too.

Can I use a keyboard? Yes. A hardware keyboard works as well as the on-screen keypad.
