Privacy Policy

Math Drop: Brain Rush · Effective 10 November 2026 · Published by Alt Solutions

Summary

Math Drop: Brain Rush does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game works fully offline, needs no account, and contains no ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, so it can remember your choices between sessions:

  • Settings: music, sound effects, vibration, reduced motion and the last difficulty you chose.
  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play tip.
  • Your best score for each starting difficulty.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the app removes it. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Android backup) may include it in device backups under that service's terms.

Permissions

On Android the game requests only the vibration permission, used for optional haptic feedback that you can turn off in the game. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access. On iOS it requests no permissions.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. App Store and Google Play may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@alt-apps.tech.
